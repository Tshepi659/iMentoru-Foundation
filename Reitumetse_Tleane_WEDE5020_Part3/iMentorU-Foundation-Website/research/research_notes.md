## Organisation
iMentorU Foundation is a South African youth development and mentorship non-profit organisation.

## Key facts used in the website
- Established in September 2017 by Mr Tinyiko Miracle Mabale.
- Officially registered in 2019.
- Target group: young people approximately 8–35 years old.
- Mission centres on empowering, mentoring and supporting children, youth and communities through mentorship, skills development and education.
- Programme pillars include Educational Support; Career Mentorship & Training; STEAM Education; Language Learning; Community & Social Development; and Sports, Recreation, Arts & Culture.
- Public contact information lists the Pretoria head office, phone numbers and email.
- Public information lists short programmes including Digital Literacy & Web Design and Business English.

## Target audience findings

The website must serve several audiences without making the navigation complicated:

- Children and young adults looking for educational, career or skills-development support.
- Parents and guardians who want to understand the organisation's purpose and programmes.
- Mentors and volunteers who want to contribute time or specialist knowledge.
- Schools, community organisations and businesses considering partnerships.
- Donors and sponsors who need contact and support information.

These findings informed the five-page structure, programme cards, visible calls to action and separate volunteer and contact forms.

## Source evaluation

The organisation's own website and profile document were used as primary sources because they provide direct information about its history, programmes, contact details and volunteering opportunities. The website content avoids presenting the demonstration forms as real application systems. Donation details are accompanied by a verification warning because financial information should be confirmed directly before payment.

## Academic-project note
The website content is based on publicly available information and is presented as an academic web-development project. Application/contact forms are demonstration forms and do not submit information to the organisation.
