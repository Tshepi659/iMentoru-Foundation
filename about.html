Who we are

Youth development with purpose.

iMentorU Foundation exists to inspire, lead, support and empower children and youth.

Our story

iMentorU Foundation NPO/NPC was established in September 2017 by Mr Tinyiko Miracle Mabale and officially registered in 2019. The organisation focuses on youth development and mentorship, with an emphasis on connecting, collaborating and building relationships that help young people.

Its target group ranges from ages 8 to 35 and includes young people who are living with disabilities, creative, innovative, unprivileged, talented or skilled.

Our mission

To empower, mentor and support children, youth and communities through mentorship, skills development and education, fostering personal growth, social responsibility and sustainable development.

Our vision

A society where every individual, especially children and youth, has access to opportunities to realise their potential and contribute positively to their communities.

Our values

What we want young people to build.

Self-confidence

Encouraging young people to recognise their abilities and speak with confidence.

Self-discipline

Supporting habits, focus and determination that contribute to long-term growth.

Dreams & goals

Helping young people turn interests, talents and ambitions into practical next steps.
